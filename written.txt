Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
The boxes stretch and can overlap. A refit keeps the same circles together, so their box has to cover the gap when they drift apart.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
The boxes fit more tightly because each rebuild groups nearby circles again. In a four-circle test, two boxes went from 104 pixels wide to 10.

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
The window cuts them off. A center can start or move within one radius of the edge, so part of the circle falls outside the image.
